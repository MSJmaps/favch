FavCH settings

Changes save as you make them.

Your channels folder

Choose the bookmark folder that holds your favorite channels. Subfolders inside it become the groups you can pick from on the feed page.

Bookmark a channel's page (or any of its videos) to add it. Organize them into subfolders however you like.

Appearance
Muted words

Videos whose titles contain any of these are hidden. One word or phrase per line; capitalization doesn't matter.

Your data

FavCH has no servers, accounts, or tracking. Your bookmarks and settings stay in this browser. It only contacts YouTube to check your channels for new videos.

Saved results

FavCH keeps each channel's recent videos for 30 minutes, and as a backup for when YouTube isn't answering. You rarely need to clear this: removed bookmarks drop out on their own.

Advanced

FavCH also remembers which channel each @handle bookmark points to. Reset this only if a channel shows another channel's videos. The next load will look up every channel again, which is slow and can make YouTube refuse requests for a while on a large folder.
